About

Sophie Utz is an accomplished legal professional based in Sydney, specialising in Banking & Finance and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) & Responsible Business law. With a strong educational foundation from the University of Sydney and advanced business studies at Oxford, Sophie brings both legal expertise and business acumen to her practice. Beyond her legal career, she is known for co-founding Australia's first intercollegiate polo program, demonstrating leadership and initiative in both professional and extracurricular pursuits.
